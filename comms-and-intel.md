# Communications & Local Intelligence

- **Family Plan:** Primary/backup rendezvous points; out‑of‑area contact.
- **Radios:** PMR/FRS channels pre‑agreed; call signs; daily check‑in time.
- **Signals:** Simple code phrases for OK/help; avoid sensitive details over air.
- **Verification:** Use two‑source rule; log reports (who/when/where).
- **Maps:** Paper maps marked with routes, clinics, water points; keep updated.
- **Neighborhood:** Skills roster; check‑in tree; elder/child welfare buddying.
- **Digital:** Prioritize end‑to‑end apps; backups of critical contacts offline.
