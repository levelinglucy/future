D) TECH & SCIENCE TIMELINE (SPINE)
This is the “infrastructure of capability” that underwrites politics and daily life.

Public health & life expectancy engines
• Clean water, sanitation, vaccination, antibiotics, refrigeration, and public health logistics.

Energy
• Fossil fuels powered the industrial surge; renewables + storage now scale as a strategic transition.
• Grid resilience becomes a national security issue (weather extremes + cyber + demand spikes).

Computing & networks
• Transistor → microprocessor → internet → smartphones → cloud.
• Satellites + GNSS become invisible critical infrastructure (timekeeping, logistics, finance).

AI
• 2010s: ML becomes widely operational; 2020s: generative AI becomes mass‑market.
• Governance pressure points: labor disruption, education, misinformation, cyber, and military applications.

Space & defense tech
• Commercial launch + mega‑constellations: communications and intelligence are increasingly “hybrid civil‑military.”
