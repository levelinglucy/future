# 30‑Day Home Resilience

- **Water:** Minimum 60–120 L/person (storage + rain capture readiness). 
- **Food:** 30 days shelf‑stable; rotate quarterly; include comfort foods.
- **Power:** UPS for modem/router; inverter or small generator with safety; solar + battery where possible.
- **Heat/Cooling:** Safe heaters/fans; blackout curtains; cool room plan; check neighbors.
- **Medical:** Extended first‑aid; chronic care supplies; air purifier.
- **Sanitation:** Hygiene kit; heavy‑duty bags; cleaning agents; bucket toilet plan.
- **Security:** Exterior lighting, door/window reinforcement, community watch.
- **Finance:** 1–3 months essential expenses; diversify access; off‑bank cash buffer.
- **Logistics:** Fuel rotation; bike/foot plans; backup cooking (stove + fuel, used safely).
