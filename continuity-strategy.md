# Worst‑Case Continuity Strategy (Multi‑Hazard Cascade)

**Triggers:** Simultaneous grid instability + supply disruption + civil unrest.

**Immediate (0–24h):**
- Safety check, headcount, triage. Activate comms plan & check‑in times.
- Shelter in place unless evacuation orders; secure water and perishables.
- Power-down non‑essential loads; move critical devices to UPS.
- Quiet posture. Lights-out discipline after dark.

**Short (Day 1–7):**
- Ration water (min 2 L/person/day) and calories (2,000 kcal/day avg).
- Neighborhood cell forms: daily sitrep; pooled watch; shared childcare/elder care.
- Rotate duties (security, water, cooking, medical, sanitation).
- Maintain logs (events, supplies, contacts).

**Medium (Week 2–4):**
- Stabilize micro‑supply chains (farmers, mills, clinics, mechanics).
- Build barter table: skills > goods. Ensure fairness and conflict mediation.
- Establish community kitchen and hygiene station.
- Rotate evac routes and fallback locations in case of new hazards.

**Recovery (Month 2+):**
- Reconnect to services; document damages; seek relief; lessons‑learned.
- Replenish stocks; repair/upgrade weak points found in drills.
